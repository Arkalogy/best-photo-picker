Best Photo Picker

Drowning in thousands of family photos? Find your best ones in minutes — scored automatically, entirely on your own Mac. No account, no cloud, nothing uploaded.

Download for Mac Apple Silicon · free

Double-click the download, drag it into Applications, and open it — that's it.

Intel Mac, Windows, or Linux? Install with pipx install "bppicker[web]" — see the install guide.

What it does

  • Scores every photo on sharpness, lighting, faces, and composition — so the good ones rise to the top.
  • Boost the people who matter. Tell it whose photos to favor — your kid, for the grandparents — and the picks follow.
  • Clears out near-duplicates so you're choosing between keepers, not burst frames.
  • Stays on your machine. No account, no subscription, no cloud upload. Open source under the MIT license.
